More questions?

How does a meal kit delivery service work?

You choose a plan and your recipes from the weekly menu. A box with pre-portioned ingredients and step-by-step recipe cards is delivered to your door.

Which meal plans are shown here?

This page shows four example plans: Meat & Veggies, Veggie, Family Friendly and Fit & Wholesome.

Is there nutrition information?

Yes. Each recipe card in a meal kit lists its nutritional information.

Can I skip a week of delivery?

Yes. As the page says above, skipping weeks or cancelling is meant to be easy.

Is this the official HelloFresh website?

No. This is a practice clone built to learn HTML, CSS and JavaScript. For real plans, prices and delivery areas, visit the official HelloFresh website.
